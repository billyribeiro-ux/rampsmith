Read with `npm view <pkg> version peerDependencies engines --json` and the crates.io API
(`/api/v1/crates/<name>`: `max_stable_version` and that version's `rust_version`), on

## Findings

- Every pin in PLAN §7 matches the registry, except `typescript`: 7.0.2 is the latest, and
  6.0.3 stays pinned because `@sveltejs/kit@3.1.0` declares `typescript ^6.0.0` and
  `svelte-check@4.7.7` declares `^5.0.0 \|\| ^6.0.0`. 6.0.3 is the newest 6.x.
- `typescript-eslint@8.71.2` accepts `typescript >=4.8.4 <6.1.0`, so 6.0.3 is inside its range.
- `openapi-typescript@7.13.0` declares `typescript ^5.x`, which excludes 6.0.3. It is first
  needed in R0 step 6; that step records the resolution in an ADR before adding it.
- The highest `rust-version` among the planned crates is 1.94.1 (`aws-sdk-s3`), below the
  pinned toolchain 1.99.1.
