```console
PASS: exactly the six seeded violations
```

`process.release.lts` is `undefined` on a Current release and the codename on an LTS release
(`Krypton` on 24.22.0, section 3 of the 24.22.0 evidence), which is what makes it a usable check.

Replacing an installed version: `npm i -g node@26.12.0` over 24.22.0 failed with
`EEXIST: file already exists` on `/home/claude/.npm-global/lib/node_modules/node/bin/node`, and
`npm uninstall -g node` removed nothing. Deleting `/home/claude/.npm-global/lib/node_modules/node`
and `/home/claude/.npm-global/bin/node` first made the install succeed.

## 6. Launch timing

From PLAN §26: "The public App Store listing is targeted for 22 March 2027", and the roadmap
line `R0 Foundations :r0, 2026-10-19, 2026-11-13`. From `schedule.json`: 24.x is in maintenance
from 2026-10-20 and reaches end of life on 2028-04-30, 13 months after the listing target; 26.x
is LTS from 2026-10-28, in maintenance from 2027-10-20 and reaches end of life on 2029-04-30.
